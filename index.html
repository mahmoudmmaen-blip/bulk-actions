Features

Everything you do to reports, done to many at once.

Every action uses the official Salesforce Analytics REST API — not screen scraping — so it keeps working through Salesforce releases.

COPY

Batch copy with smart names

Clone many reports in one go. Name copies with a pattern like {name} - Q4; names stay within Salesforce’s 40-character limit and duplicates get “(2)”, “(3)” automatically.

MOVE

Batch move to any folder

Pick a destination folder, move everything at once, and undo for 30 seconds if you picked the wrong one.

DELETE

Safe batch delete

Clean up old reports with a JSON backup downloaded first, a 30-second undo, and a clear confirm step. Reports used on dashboards are skipped.

SEARCH

Typo-tolerant search

Press Ctrl+Shift+F anywhere in Salesforce. Finds “Pipeline” even when you type “pipline”. Filter by folder, creator and date.

RESTORE

Restore from backup

Missed the undo window? Load a backup file and recreate the reports you need, with per-report results.

PRIVATE

Your data stays yours

OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, tokens encrypted in your browser, no report data sent to us. English and Arabic interface.

How it works

Set up once in about 10 minutes.

Install the extension

Add it to Chrome or Edge from the Chrome Web Store.

Connect your org

Create a Salesforce External Client App (step-by-step guide included), paste its Consumer Key, and sign in with Salesforce.

Select and act

Open Reports in Lightning, tick the reports you want, and use the Copy, Move or Delete bar at the bottom of the page.

FAQ

Questions admins ask.

Does it work with Salesforce Classic?

No. It is built for Lightning Experience (the Reports list at /lightning/o/Report/home).

Can I undo a delete?

Yes, for 30 seconds with one click. After that, deleted reports stay in the Salesforce Recycle Bin for 15 days, and you also get a JSON backup file. Restoring from undo or backup creates new reports with the same settings; restoring from the Recycle Bin keeps the original ID.

Do you store my Salesforce data?

No. The extension talks directly to your Salesforce org from your browser. We do not run a server that receives your reports. See the privacy policy.

What permissions do I need in Salesforce?

API access and permission to create, edit or delete the reports and folders you act on. The extension can’t do anything your Salesforce user can’t already do.

Can my whole team use one license?

One Pro license covers one person on up to 3 browsers. Each admin needs their own license.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, from the link in your receipt email or the “Manage subscription” button in Settings → Account. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
